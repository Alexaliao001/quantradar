Questions before buying

What does the replay show?

Reconstructed mechanical posture from dated prices. Historical earnings and sector gates are unavailable. It is not a record of previously issued signals, investment returns or a backtest of executable trades.

What if some data is missing?

Unavailable or inconsistent data stays UNKNOWN. A watchlist report can be partial. Single-stock report checkout opens only after the required history is available.

How do cancellation and refunds work?

Use “Manage or cancel subscription” above. Cancellation stops renewal; existing paid access continues to its scheduled end unless refunded. Downloaded and saved reports remain available according to the refund policy.

Does website Pro unlock the iOS app?

No. They are independent products: website plans use Stripe and iOS Unlock uses Apple In-App Purchase.

Will this improve my returns?

That is not guaranteed. QuantRadar helps you apply a consistent research checklist. It does not recommend trades, manage money or promise profitable results.
