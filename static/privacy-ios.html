Privacy Policy — iOS app

Last updated: 2026-08-13 · Fortune Insight, LLC · App: QuantRadar (one.quantradar.app)

This page covers the iOS App Store product only. The website at quantradar.one has a separate privacy policy because it uses accounts and payments.

What the iOS app collects

We do not collect personal data from the iOS app.

  • No account, email, or sign-in.
  • No analytics SDK, advertising ID, or tracking.
  • Tickers you scan or watch stay on device (UserDefaults / local cache).
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your card number.

Network

The app fetches public market bars from free quote endpoints (currently Yahoo Finance and Nasdaq) to compute an on-device posture score. Those requests include the ticker you typed. We do not operate a user database for iOS scans.

Notifications

Optional local notifications fire on this device when a watched ticker’s posture changes. They are scheduled on-device. We do not send server push.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use the iOS app to identify you on the website.
  • Website subscriptions do not unlock the iOS app, and iOS unlock does not sign you into the website.

Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact channel listed on quantradar.one.
